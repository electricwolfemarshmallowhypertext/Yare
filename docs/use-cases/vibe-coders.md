# Vibe Coders

Use case:
Safe next step for AI-built projects

Who it is for:
Nontraditional builders using AI tools to ship apps, scripts, websites, and prototypes.

What breaks today:
They ask AI to build fast, then lose track of what changed, what broke, what is real, and what to do next.

Why Yare fits:
Yare turns messy AI build sessions into a plain handoff a person can understand.

What Yare stores:
- what changed
- what is true
- what is unverified
- contradictions
- open loops
- human approval items
- next clean action
- receipts

What Roach makes durable:
CockroachDB keeps the project memory safe across sessions, tools, and restarts.

What the user sees:
A simple answer:
"Here is what happened. Here is what is safe. Here is what still needs checking. Do this next."

When they use it:
- after a long AI coding session
- before asking another AI to continue
- before deploying
- after something breaks
- when switching tools
- when returning to a project days later

Why it matters:
Vibe coders move fast, but blind speed creates broken projects. Yare gives them a safety rail without making them become senior engineers overnight.

Demo proof:
Yare reads prior AI runs, saves the state in CockroachDB, and prints a current-state handoff.

One-line pitch:
Yare helps vibe coders know what the AI actually changed and what to do next.
