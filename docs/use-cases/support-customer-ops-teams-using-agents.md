# Support / Customer Ops Teams Using Agents

Use case:
Support knowledge handoff

Who it is for:
Support and customer ops teams using AI agents to update help docs, bug notes, escalation summaries, customer issue records, and internal runbooks.

What breaks today:
Agents summarize issues, suggest fixes, update docs, and write notes, but the team loses track of what was verified, what was assumed, and what still needs escalation.

Why Yare fits:
Yare turns support-agent work into a clear handoff with proof, unresolved claims, and next action.

What Yare stores:
- files or notes changed
- verified customer facts
- unverified claims
- contradictions
- escalation items
- approval items
- receipts
- next clean action

What Roach makes durable:
CockroachDB keeps the support memory available across shifts, agents, teammates, and repeated customer issues.

What the user sees:
A support handoff:
"What happened, what is confirmed, what is still unclear, who needs to approve, and what to do next."

When they use it:
- during shift handoff
- after an AI support summary
- after a bug escalation
- before updating help docs
- when a customer issue reopens
- when multiple agents touched the same issue

Why it matters:
Support teams need continuity. Yare reduces repeated investigation and bad AI summaries.

Demo proof:
Yare's handoff format already captures verified facts, unverified claims, contradictions, human approval items, and next clean action.

One-line pitch:
Yare helps support teams keep AI-assisted customer work clear, reviewable, and durable.
