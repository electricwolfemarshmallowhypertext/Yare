# Content/Research Operators

Use case:
AI-assisted content and research handoff

Who it is for:
Newsletter, podcast, research, editorial, and publishing teams using AI to draft, summarize, fact-check, and revise work.

What breaks today:
AI creates drafts, claims, summaries, edits, and source notes across different tools. The team loses track of what is verified, what is questionable, and what still needs review.

Why Yare fits:
Yare turns scattered AI content work into one reviewable state.

What Yare stores:
- changed drafts or files
- verified claims
- unverified claims
- contradictions
- source/checking notes
- approval items
- open loops
- next clean action

What Roach makes durable:
CockroachDB keeps the research and review state available across writers, editors, agents, and publishing steps.

What the user sees:
A plain editorial handoff:
"What changed, what is checked, what needs sourcing, what conflicts, and what to review next."

When they use it:
- before publishing
- after AI research runs
- after edits from multiple tools
- during fact-checking
- when handing work from writer to editor
- when returning to a draft later

Why it matters:
AI can produce content quickly, but teams still need truth, sourcing, and review discipline.

Demo proof:
Yare's handoff format already tracks verified facts, unverified claims, contradictions, approval items, and next action.

One-line pitch:
Yare helps content and research teams keep AI-assisted work reviewable before it goes public.
